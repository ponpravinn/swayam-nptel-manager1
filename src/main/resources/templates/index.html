Core Capabilities

Built Specifically for NPTEL MOOC Students

Overcoming the challenges of non-extendable weekly deadlines and dispersed academic notifications.

Automated Deadline Tracking

Tracks 12-week assignment schedules with live countdowns. Dynamic color-coded badges immediately alert you of pending, completed, or overdue tasks.

WhatsApp & Email Alerts

Receive instant reminders directly on WhatsApp. Includes 1-click WhatsApp share buttons to forward weekly deadlines to college peer study groups.

Tamil Nadu Colleges & Branches

Searchable directory featuring 200+ Tamil Nadu institutions (IIT Madras, NIT Trichy, Anna University, PSG, SSN, CIT, VIT) with dedicated Department & Branch selectors.

Separate OTP Verification

Double contact verification during student registration with separate 6-digit Email and WhatsApp/SMS OTP confirmation buttons and live demo auto-fill support.

TCS iON Exam Tracker

Track official NPTEL in-person examination dates, Morning & Afternoon shift timings, test centers, fee payment milestones, and hall ticket downloads.

NPTEL Certificate Predictor

Simulates authentic IIT/IISc certification formulas: Best 8 of 12 assignments (25%) + Proctored Exam (75%) with Elite, Silver, and Gold medal band predictions.
